**Profile and city presets**
Pick one of 19 Polish cities, your type of home and your age. The dashboard fills in typical costs for that city: rent (April 2026 offer prices), bills, food, car and other costs.

**Monthly budget with sliders**
Change your costs (investments, food, rent and bills, loan, car and fuel, other) and your income. Enter your salary as net or gross. The dashboard works out ZUS, health insurance and income tax for an employment contract, using 2026 rules.

**PIT-0 tracker (under 26)**
Shows the month you go over the 85,528 PLN tax-free limit, your take-home pay before and after, and how much tax comes back in the yearly return. If you turn 26 this year, it shows when the relief ends. From age 26 it simply shows how much you take home in the whole year.

**Monthly balance**
Shows what is left each month, how your pay is split between costs, and your savings rate. Warns you if costs are higher than income.

**Bank account**
Set your balance on 1 January and see how it changes month by month.

**Net worth**
Add your own investments, other assets (like a car or a flat) and liabilities (like a loan). Each one has its own value, monthly payment and yearly rate. A chart shows assets, liabilities and net worth for each month.

**Investment forecast**
Shows how your investments can grow over 1 to 30 years with compound return.

**Tax relief finder**
Enter any year from 2019. The dashboard works out your age in that year and shows which Polish tax reliefs fit you: PIT-0, IKZE, IKE, child relief, internet relief, higher work costs, author costs and more. Where it can, it estimates how much you save. On claude.ai you can also ask Claude for a personal answer.

**Other**
- Polish and English
- Light and dark mode, works on phone and desktop
- Starts clean every time, so your numbers are not saved
- One HTML file with plain JavaScript and SVG charts, no libraries

This is a planning tool, not tax advice.
